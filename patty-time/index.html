Know before you go

  • 💵
    Cash only (according to reviewers). A Google review from about a month ago says “they only take cash,” and reviews on Restaurantji, Yelp and other sites say the same. The shop doesn’t state it anywhere we could read, and one directory lists debit, so bring cash to be safe.
  • ⏱️
    They can close early. The sign on the door says closing time “may on occasion be slightly earlier than expected should we be sold out of patties and food.”
  • 🧾
    Keep your receipt. The same notice asks customers to check their receipt and have it ready when their number is called.
  • 🌅
    Go early. “It can get very busy so the earlier you go the better,” one Google reviewer says. Saturdays around noon to 2 p.m. are usually the busiest.
  • 🥡
    Takeout only. It’s a counter, and plenty of reviewers’ photos were snapped in the car on the way home.
